COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

For advice about your own horse, call the practice directly.

Should I keep riding a horse that has become lame?

Do not continue exercise if your horse appears lame. Contact your vet for individual guidance.

Can imaging be carried out at my yard?

The practice advertises mobile diagnostic equipment. Suitability depends on the examination and conditions at the yard; please ask when booking.
