Understanding movement with Equinosis Q.

Equinosis Q is an objective gait analysis system. Sensors record movement while a horse is assessed, helping a vet quantify asymmetry and compare findings over time. The results support clinical judgement; they do not, on their own, identify the cause of lameness.

What we can help with

  • Objective measurements of movement asymmetry during a gait assessment
  • Additional information to support a clinical lameness work-up
  • A way to compare recorded findings when reassessment is appropriate
  • Interpretation alongside examination and, if needed, diagnostic imaging

Whether Equinosis analysis is appropriate depends on the horse and the clinical concern. Read about our lameness investigations or contact the practice to discuss availability.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

For advice about your own horse, please contact the practice directly.

Is Equinosis Q the same as a lameness diagnosis?

No. It objectively measures aspects of gait asymmetry, but a veterinary examination and sometimes other tests are needed to understand the cause.

Can the system be used for every horse?

Not always. Your veterinary team will advise whether Equinosis Q assessment is appropriate and available for your horse.
