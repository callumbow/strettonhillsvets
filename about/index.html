A PRACTICE WITH A PURPOSE

The individual matters.

Stretton Hills Veterinary Practice is based at Dudgeley Farm in All Stretton, near Church Stretton. Our work is focused on horses and their wellbeing, from routine preventative appointments to more involved clinical care.

Our aim is to make veterinary conversations practical and useful. We want owners to understand the choices available, the reasoning behind our advice and what happens next.

As a countryside-based practice, we recognise that horses are cared for in real-world settings. Please contact the team to discuss on-yard visits, current services and appointment availability.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Good judgment. Genuine care.

We take a considered, horse-first approach: look closely, listen carefully and recommend what is appropriate for the case at hand.

  • Dedicated equine focus
  • A practical approach to veterinary advice
  • Routine and diagnostic services
  • Out-of-hours emergency provision
