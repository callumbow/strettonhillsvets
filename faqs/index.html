PRACTICAL INFORMATION

We are here to help.

Can't find what you need? One phone call is often the simplest way forward.

Call 01694 751748
Where is Stretton Hills Veterinary Practice based?

We are based at Dudgeley Farm, Lower Wood, All Stretton, Church Stretton, Shropshire SY6 6LE.

What are your normal opening hours?

The RCVS lists our regular hours as Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm. Saturday and Sunday are emergencies only.

What should I do in an equine emergency?

Telephone 01694 751748 immediately for current instructions. Do not use email or wait for a website response.

Do you only treat horses?

The practice is listed by the RCVS as treating horses and is dedicated to equine veterinary care.

Can you visit my yard?

The practice has published information about ambulatory equine services. Call us with your yard location to confirm availability and arrangements.

Can I book an appointment online?

Appointments are currently arranged by contacting the practice by telephone or email. For urgent matters, please telephone.

Can I get a quote before a service?

Yes, contact the team to ask about current fees and how a particular appointment or procedure is charged. Costs may depend on the case and visit requirements.

Do you offer pre-purchase examinations?

Yes, pre-purchase vettings are among the practice’s advertised services. Contact the team to discuss location, timing and examination type.

What if I am unsure which service my horse needs?

You do not need to make a diagnosis yourself. Call the practice and explain your concerns so they can advise on an appropriate next step.
