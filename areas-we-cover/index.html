LOCAL, PRACTICAL CARE

Based in All Stretton.
Here for horse owners.

Stretton Hills Veterinary Practice is located at Dudgeley Farm, Lower Wood, All Stretton, near Church Stretton. Our published practice information describes an ambulatory equine service covering Shropshire, Staffordshire and neighbouring counties.

Visit availability, call-out distance and appointment arrangements can vary. For a new client or a yard outside the immediate area, please telephone us to confirm coverage before making plans.
